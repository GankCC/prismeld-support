PRISMELD Support

To play, drag through at least three touching gems of the same color. You can drag diagonally. Lift your finger to clear the gems and score points. Open How to play from the home screen or during a game to practice.

Game progress is stored on your device. To see the app version and build number, open Settings and look for Version.

Contact support

Email suphasit.tn@gmail.com with your device model, app version, level or game mode, and what happened. You can attach a screenshot if it helps explain the issue.
